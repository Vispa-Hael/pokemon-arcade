# Mystery-game trivia

`trivia.json` contains English Pokédex flavor-text entries from the existing cached PokéAPI `pokemon-species` responses. Species IDs are the keys; each fact retains its originating game-version name. Source: https://pokeapi.co/docs/v2/#pokemon-species and `.cache/pokeapi/pokemon-species_<id>.json`. The data attribution and retained license are in `docs/POKEAPI-LICENSE.md`. Whitespace, soft line-wrap hyphens and Pokémon capitalization were normalized. Entries containing the species name were excluded to avoid directly giving away the answer. The playable seven-species roster has four facts each. No network calls are made when revealing trivia.

Types are free. Trivia costs 10 points per hint, misses cost 15 points. Reveal more removes the silhouette mask and applies a single 50% multiplier to the final winning score after those deductions, rounded down. Losses still score zero. A fresh round clears the reveal penalty; restored rounds retain it. Version-1 saves without the optional reveal flag remain supported.

National Dex expansion: facts are now selected from English flavor-text rows in the cached bulk table `pokemon_species_flavor_text.csv`, joined to `versions.csv`, for all 1,025 species. Existing curated seed facts were retained. Each entry is still limited to available source material, with a maximum of four. Where English text repeats the answer or is unavailable, a sourced species classification can serve as the only clue. Exact availability is in `docs/CHARACTER-COVERAGE.json`. Corpus/source fingerprints are retained there. The prior seven-species/four-facts-each description above records the initial version only.
